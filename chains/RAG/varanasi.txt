Varanasi is the upcoming epic action-adventure film directed by S. S. Rajamouli, starring Mahesh Babu, Priyanka Chopra Jonas, and Prithviraj Sukumaran. The film is scheduled for a worldwide theatrical release on April 7, 2027.

Blending mythology, time travel, adventure, science-fiction elements, and powerful human emotion, Varanasi promises to be one of the most ambitious Indian films of 2027. Mahesh Babu plays Rudhra, while Priyanka Chopra portrays Mandakini and Prithviraj Sukumaran plays Kumbha.

The story is being kept largely secret, but Rajamouli has described the film as a journey involving Antarctica, the Ramayana, gods, natural disasters, and extraordinary fantasy elements, with the emotional core centered on the relationship between a father and his son.

The film is being made on a massive visual scale and is being shot using IMAX-certified digital cameras. Rajamouli has also confirmed that Varanasi is planned as a single standalone film, rather than the beginning of a multi-part franchise.

With M. M. Keeravani composing the music and Rajamouli bringing together mythology, spectacular action and emotional storytelling, Varanasi is shaping up to be one of the most highly anticipated cinematic events of 2027.

Release Date: April 7, 2027
Director: S. S. Rajamouli
Lead Cast: Mahesh Babu, Priyanka Chopra Jonas, Prithviraj Sukumaran
Music: M. M. Keeravani
Genre: Action • Adventure • Fantasy • Mythology • Time Travel
